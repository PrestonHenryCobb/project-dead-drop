# Map brief: The Gala (rework of the layout, Step 7B)

A design brief to correct before anything is rebuilt. The reference screenshots set the mood and the shape language only.
No layout, name or asset from another game is copied (spec IP note).

**What changes.** The tower stays, but it stops being a box. The building becomes a sunlit glass-and-timber **spire rising
out of a garden estate**: open courts, a pond with a footbridge, roof gardens, bridges and boardwalks join its wings.
Roughly half of everything you walk through is open air.

**Still true from your earlier answers.** About 450 x 450 studs, five main levels with split levels and balconies, a tall
central atrium, three vaults with climbing tier doors, hard-gated nested guard rooms two levels deep, three outdoor
extraction sites (helipad, loading dock, garden lawn), 90 NPCs (Guest 40, Staff 18, Security 16, Executive 16), night with
lit areas, about 15,000 parts for the finished map, and a 15 to 25 stud average room so nothing is a huge empty box.

---

## The idea in one paragraph

Arrive at dusk through the gardens of a hilltop estate. A pink-and-amber tower climbs in glass terraces above a koi pond
and a waterfall. Inside, a skylit atrium ties four floors together, so you can look down on the party from a balcony or up
at the stars. The public side glows amber and smells of cocktails; the staff side is brick and fluorescent; the security
wing is cold teal; the penthouse is deep purple velvet. Guests drift across bridges between buildings. Everything
important is either on the roof, behind a glass wall, or under the water garden.

## Colour and mood by tier (zone readability)

| Tier | Where | Look |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Public | gardens, atrium, ballroom, galleries | warm amber light, honey timber, polished patterned floors, planters, tall glass |
| 1 Staff | kitchens, offices, catering, coat check | brick, tile and fluorescent strips, blue carpet, red accents, narrower corridors |

## The grounds (outdoors, Tier 0)

**1. The Arrival Court (forecourt).** A curved drive swings round a fountain with a bronze statue. Valet kiosks, lantern
posts and a long glass canopy lead to the tower doors. This is the main insertion area and the first thing a team sees.

**2. The Koi Pond Garden.** The pond court: a pond with lily pads and glowing lamps, a red timber footbridge, stone
lanterns and a waterfall pouring from a terrace above. Trees cast soft shade. Crowds of Guests stroll the paths. Hiding
spots: behind lanterns, under the bridge, in the planter beds. Gates connect it to the arrival court and the garden lawn.

**3. The Garden Lawn (extraction site).** A wide, open lawn with a pergola, picnic umbrellas and low hedges. It has long
sight lines, so reaching it is a gamble. A boardwalk deck runs along its edge under a diamond-lattice glass pavilion.

**4. The Glass Pavilion Deck.** The boardwalk and pavilion from the references: shallow reflecting pools, cafe tables and
a triangular glass roof you can walk beside. A social hub with good cover.

**5. The Loading Dock (extraction site).** A rear service yard fenced off from the gardens: truck bays, pallets, a
forklift lane and a roller door into the kitchens. Grittier, grey and sodium-yellow, with the best sneak routes in.

**6. The Hedge Maze and Orchard.** A maze of hedges and an orchard with benches, tight turns and lamp-lit alcoves.
The place to lose a pursuer, and to be surprised.

**7. The Pool Terrace (rear).** An infinity-edge pool with loungers and a pool bar on the north side. Two bridges cross
the water.

**Connections.** Hedged courts are joined by gates. Two **skybridges** link tower wings across the gardens at the
mezzanine, and a **zipline** drops from the roof to the lawn (cover-breaking).

## The tower, from the ground up

**Basement (B): the Service Underbelly.** Storage halls and security corridors. Pipes, crates, stacked pallets, cold
fluorescent light. **Vault A** sits in the core, behind an armory antechamber.

**Ground (G): the Grand Level.**
- **The Skylit Atrium.** A four-storey hall with a glass roof, a glass elevator in a brown column, a ring of balconies,
  trailing plants, round tables and a patterned floor of amber octagons. The heart of the party and the biggest crowd.
- **The Ballroom.** Opens off the atrium: dance floor, a stage, long buffet tables, and pillars that break up sight lines.
- **The Cocktail Bar.** A long glowing counter, hanging lamps, bar stools and booths. Always busy.
- **The Smoking Lounge.** Dim leather chairs, a humidor (T1 nested), and a garden door to the lawn.
- **The Sculpture Hall.** Freestanding plinths and low partitions, ideal for stalking.
- **The Kitchen and Catering Prep.** Brick walls, steel islands, steam, a dock door and a pool-side back door.
- **The Coat Check and Concierge.** A small, busy staff front of house.
- **Vault C wing.** A vault antechamber and the vault behind the catering corridor.

**Mezzanine (M): the Galleries.**
- **The Grand Gallery.** A long hall of partitions, framed art and statues, with a terrace along one side.
- **The Masquerade Hall.** Draped, candle-lit, mask displays, a stage and columns. Masks are worn here until 5:00.
- **The Atrium Balcony.** A railed walkway round the void, with views down to the party and up to the glass roof.
- **Offices.** The red-curtain open-plan office from the references: blue patterned carpet, long desks, window walls
  with a view of the roof gardens. The security checkpoint is nested here.
- **The Security Hub.** Camera walls, glass-walled monitor rooms, and the Executive records room (T3 nested).
- **The Mezzanine Terrace.** A garden terrace ring with planters, benches and a skybridge to the east wing.

**Top Floor (T): the Penthouse Level.**
- **The Great Room.** Deep purple, brass lamps, a grand piano, a balcony over the atrium void.
- **The Private Gallery.** Spot-lit sculptures and paintings.
- **Guard halls (west and east).** Cool teal corridors with glass-walled guard rooms; the **panic room** and **executive
  office** are nested T3 rooms.
- **Vault B wing.** An antechamber and vault behind the great room.
- **The Upper Terrace.** A narrow garden ring with a plunge pool and cocktail tables.

**Roof (R): the Rooftop Cocktail Party (extraction site).** The glass-railed roof garden: trees in beds, an infinity
pool, a bar, string lights, cabanas and a helipad pad at one end. The crowd is thick; the sky is wide.

## What makes it feel less boxed in

- **Glass, not blank walls.** Glass-railed terraces and glass-walled rooms (guard rooms, cafe, offices) so you can see
  across spaces, and be seen.
- **Open voids.** The atrium and two garden courts cut right through the tower; you can look down on the party from
  three floors.
- **Rounded and angled forms.** Curved terrace edges, triangular pavilion roofs, an octagonal floor pattern, a drum
  elevator, instead of straight boxes.
- **Bridges.** Skybridges, a footbridge and boardwalks give routes that are not corridors.
- **Water and trees.** A pond, a waterfall, pools and 60 or more trees for cover and softness.
- **Nested secrets.** Guard rooms inside staff rooms inside the party, behind clearance doors.

## Routes and tension

- **Three ways up** besides the main stair: the staff service stairs (T1), the security stairs (T2) and the exterior
  garden steps and bridges (T0).
- **Three ways in** to every vault, including at least one outdoor approach.
- **Sight lines** are broken by planters, columns, pergolas and partitions. Only two or three are longer than 150 studs
  (the lawn, the roof, and the atrium void).
- **Choke points** are the clearance doors: you either hold the right disguise, tailgate, or take the long way.

## NPC life (90)

| Area | Crowd |
|---|---|
| Atrium, ballroom, bar, pond garden, rooftop | the thick crowds (about 45 Guests and Staff between them) |
| Galleries, masquerade hall, pavilion deck, lawn | medium, groups at art, walking pairs |
| Kitchen, offices, dock | Staff at work in clusters |
| Guard halls, hub, security corridors | Security on posts and loops (16) |
| Penthouse, private gallery, vault wings | Executives in slow walks (16), a few in each room |

Density is a weight on each room, tuned by playtest, and no room is empty for long in the early match.

## Performance

- Graybox first: boxes and a few mesh-like primitives; the art pass swaps in meshes and textures.
- Glass is a simple transparent part with a cheap material. No real-time reflections.
- Trees are one trunk and a ball canopy; water is a flat coloured part with an animated texture later.
- A part budget per area is tracked in the generator's self-check, and StreamingEnabled is on with a 250 stud radius.

pond court with statue, waterfall and lanterns, the belvedere deck with a ramp up from the lawn, a glass-and-timber
pavilion boardwalk on the lawn, 110 interior lights, and 90 NPCs spread by role and tier.

## Revision (2026-10-23): a tower on top of a skyscraper

This replaces the grounds in the sections above. There is no outdoor ground level any more: the ground floor is entirely
inside the tower, with windows onto a cityscape that lies far below. The koi pond court, the arrival court, the hedge maze,
the pool terrace, the valet lane, the service yard and the outdoor loading dock are gone. The only outdoor spaces are the
mezzanine and upper terraces, the roof, the skybridge and the lawn deck. See the table in BUILD_ORDER.md, Step 7B.
