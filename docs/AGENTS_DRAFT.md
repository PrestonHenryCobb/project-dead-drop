# Agent roster: draft for review (2026-10-23)

Six agents (SPEC_CHANGES: 6 agents, 3 free and 3 unlockable, 3 weapons each). Each has a distinct name and silhouette, **one
ability on a cooldown** (key `Q`) and **one passive** that defines how they play. The spec's separate "cover perk" is folded
into the passive. There is no passive healing in the game except where an agent's kit says so (Ozzy).

Built from the spec's eight (Section 15). Rook and Ferro are cut: Rook's mines overlap Sable and Juno, and Ferro's grapple
overlaps the new ziplines, gondola and vents. Their best ideas are kept as gadgets or assets for later.

| Agent | Role | Ability (cooldown) | Passive | Signature weapons (3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Vesper**, the Chameleon | Disguise master | **Quick Change** (30 s): instantly switch to her stored disguise, even in combat lockout; not while tracked by Security or carrying the Package. | **Two Faces**: two disguise slots, and scanning takes 0.6 s instead of 1.2 s. | Suppressed SMG, silenced pistol, garrote (melee) |
| **Halden**, the Bulwark | Frontline anchor | **Riot Wall** (35 s): a carried energy shield, 8 x 6 studs, 400 HP, 8 s. He fires through it; others can't. | **Plated**: starts every life with 2 armor plates, and puts plates on twice as fast. No disguise bonuses. | Pump shotgun, heavy SMG, breaching hammer |
| **Juno**, the Technician | Hacker and scout | **Sparrow Drone** (40 s): fly a drone for 15 s that hacks stations, cameras and doors remotely and tags agents in True Form for her team (8 s). 40 HP. | **Wired**: every hack (intel stations, doors, cameras, the vault) is 25% faster, and tier doors cost her 1 less intel (never below 1). | Burst rifle, compact PDW, taser |
| **Sable**, the Marksman | Overwatch | **Tracer Glass** (30 s): for 6 s her scope highlights any Exposed agent, and anyone over 50% suspicion, in a 45 degree cone to 150 studs. | **Still Water**: scoping while disguised raises no suspicion until she fires; falls hurt half as much. | Bolt-action DMR, marksman pistol, folding carbine |
| **Ozzy**, the Field Medic | Sustain (the only healer) | **Mend Field** (45 s): a canister that heals allies in 10 studs by 12 HP/s for 8 s. Disguised in a public zone it passes as a first-aid kit; elsewhere it breaks Cover. | **Triage**: teammates within 12 studs regain 2 HP/s after 6 s out of combat, and he revives in 2 s instead of 4. | Dual machine pistols, medic shotgun, flare pistol |
| **Mirage**, the Illusionist | Misdirection | **Double** (30 s): a hologram of her current look walks her aim line for 8 s, acting like an NPC if disguised; 1 HP, gives a red hitmarker, then pops. | **Smoke and Mirrors**: when she is Exposed, the ping shows 8 to 15 studs away from her, and re-disguising takes 0.3 s. | Heavy revolver, smoke-burst SMG, throwing knives |

**Free:** Vesper, Halden, Juno. **Unlock with Credits:** Sable, Ozzy, Mirage.
