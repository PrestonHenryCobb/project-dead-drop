## Furniture groups (owner 2026-11-22: rooms still empty, items that did not go together)
The reference repo (isaiahagi/roblox-ai) is a library of Roblox game thumbnails and UI mockups, with no interior layouts, so nothing was copied from it; the only idea taken is
that props sit in functional groups. Lone random tables and desks are replaced by groups placed as one (`Dress.group` in `Tools/Gala/Build.luau`): dining sets (table, cloth,
centrepiece, six chairs), cocktail sets (round table with stools), lounge sets (sofa, armchairs, coffee table, lamp on a rug), desk pods (two desks facing, chairs, screens), casino
tables (felt table, player and dealer stools), gallery displays (plinth, case, exhibit, rope), kitchen stations, and bar stools. Rooms are assigned in the `PROPS` override in
`tools/relayout-layout.cjs`; per-room caps are in the `DRESS` table in Build.luau. Each group registers a task stop (Talk, Work, Art, Lean) beside it. All are placeholder blocks.
Built in Studio: 0 problems, about 13.5k parts, 160 chairs.

## Fabs and room recipes (owner 2026-11-22: fill every room with fabs that make sense, whether or not an art pass comes)
Furnishing is now data-driven. `src/server/Tools/Gala/Fabs.luau` holds about 70 named placeholder fabs (chairs, sofas, dining sets, desk pods, bunks, shelving rows,
washers, boilers, beds, stalls, slot islands, outdoor lounges, and so on) and one recipe per room type (dining, library, lobby, kitchen, barracks, suite, casino, terrace...).
`tools/relayout-layout.cjs` assigns a recipe to each room (`FURNISH`). A room's props run in this order: structural pieces (pillars, reception desk, bar counter, planters, trees),
its recipe, then wall decor (sconces, paintings, banners, chandeliers). Intel, safes and the other placements are made first so furniture cannot take their wall spots.
`Build.luau` provides the placement helpers (`Dress.alongWalls` for back-to-the-wall fabs, `Dress.group` for free-standing fabs with aisles) and logs `furnish <room>: fab placed/wanted`
for every room. Furniture keeps `DRESS.ReservedMargin` further from doorways, stairs and windows, and each fab registers an NPC task stop (Talk, Sit, Work, Lean, Art, Drink).
An art pass can swap a fab's parts for a model by id without touching placement.
Built in Studio 2026-11-22: 0 problems, about 16.8k parts (the old budget was about 15k; StreamingEnabled should be on), 961 NPC nodes (limit 1000).
Known thin rooms (the log shows them): casino (8 table and 8 island wanted, fewer fit between the pillars), the bars, library lounge sets, restroom stalls, terrace parasol tables.

## Thin, empty, floating and colliding items (owner 2026-11-22)
- **Fixes:** `ReservedMargin` 3 -> 1 and tighter aisles let furniture fill rooms that were being refused (most rejections were reserved zones: door strips, stair clearance);
  restrooms use single stalls and basins that fit their short walls; the Sanctum lounge set is gone (it touched the Package pedestal); the boiler corridor keeps wider aisles (tight boilers
  cut a pocket off); the mezzanine terrace strips stay bare (furniture sealed those 15-stud walkways); wide wall fabs keep further from corners.
- **Floating:** a wall piece now needs real solid wall behind it along its whole width (`wallBehind`, three rays), so nothing hangs at an open colonnade, arch or railing. Carts have wheels;
  weapon racks, boards and pipe runs stand on feet or posts down to the floor.
- **Checks:** every fab part carries `Fab` and `FabIdx`; wall fabs and wall decor carry `WallBack`/`WallOrigin`/`WallDist` or `WallNormal`. A Studio script (see this session) tests floating, sunk, away-from-wall and overlapping.
  Result on the final build: no wall fab away from a wall, no floating or sunk fab, no fab overlapping another, four cashier-counter overlaps with a wall, three decor pieces away from a wall on the upper floor.
  The other overlaps the check reports (intel stations, safes, cameras, trusses, fountain rims) are the map's own fixtures embedded in walls by design.
- **Built:** 0 problems, about 21.3k parts, 875 NPC nodes, a 38 to 90 second build.
